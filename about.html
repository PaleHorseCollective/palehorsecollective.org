About

Pale Horse Collective is a small, independent clothing label. Every design starts with a symbol you've probably walked past a thousand times without thinking about it: the pyramid on the dollar bill, a broadcast tower, an eagle with two heads.

We look into where each one came from and what it was used for, then draw our own version and print it on a tee. The clothes come first. The stories are there if you want them.

We post the research behind each design on TikTok, and the longer write-ups live in the stories archive.

Shipping

Shipping is free on every order. Each piece is printed to order once you buy it, so allow 10 to 14 days for it to reach you.

Returns

Because everything is printed to order, we can't take returns for change of mind. If your order arrives damaged or misprinted, email us within 30 days with a photo and we'll replace it.
